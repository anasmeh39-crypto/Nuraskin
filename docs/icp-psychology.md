## Psychographic Profile

### What She Believes
- "كانت جدتي تعتني ببشرتها بزاف" — skincare is a cultural value, not vanity
- Good skin = confidence, not superficiality
- Imported brands are expensive and not designed for her skin
- She's been burned by products that promised everything and delivered nothing
- COD is the only safe way to try something new online

### What She Fears
- Losing money on a product that doesn't work
- Being scammed by an online store (ghash)
- Using something that breaks her skin out
- Looking like she tried too hard
- Her money being taken before she sees the product

### What She Wants
- Skin that looks naturally clear, rested, and glowing
- A simple routine she can actually stick to
- A brand that feels premium but doesn't condescend
- Products that understand Moroccan heat, humidity, and skin types
- To feel like she made a smart, sophisticated choice

### What Motivates Her
- Social proof from people who look like her
- Before/after perception (not fake transformations)
- Ingredient transparency (she's increasingly educated)
- Limited-time offers that feel genuine, not manipulative
- Routine-building language (the "ritual" frame)

---

## Decision Journey

### Awareness
She sees a video on TikTok or Reels showing someone with beautiful skin using Nura Balance. The copy is in Darija. It doesn't feel like an ad. She watches twice.

### Consideration
She visits the website. Checks the ingredients. Reads the Arabic copy. Looks for reviews. Checks if COD is available. Reads the return policy. Looks at the price.

### Intent
She adds to cart. Then hesitates. Common objections:
- "غادي يجي ولا لا؟" (Will it actually arrive?)
- "واش هادا رايح يخدم معايا؟" (Will it work for me?)
- "شحال كيتكلف التوصيل؟" (How much is delivery?)
- "واش ممكن نرجعها؟" (Can I return it?)

### Purchase
Checkout popup answers all 4 objections before she closes. COD framing removes payment risk. Scarcity is present but not fake. She places the order.

### Post-Purchase
She waits. She receives a confirmation message. The package arrives well-packaged. The product smells premium. She starts using it. She comes back.

## COD Psychology

COD is not a limitation — it's a trust mechanism.

- "ما تدفعي حتى يوصلك" is the most powerful CRO sentence in Morocco
- COD signals: we trust you to pay when you see the product
- It removes the #1 barrier to first purchase
- Position COD as a feature, not a fallback
- Reinforce in: hero, cart drawer, checkout popup, thank-you page

---

## Retention Psychology

- Frame each product as part of a ritual system
- Use cross-sells to build the complete routine
- Thank-you page reduces cancellation anxiety
- Packaging quality creates unboxing satisfaction
- Re-order prompt in post-delivery follow-up (WhatsApp/SMS future)
