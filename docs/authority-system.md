# Nura Skin — Authority System

## What Authority Means for Nura Skin

Authority is not about fake credentials or fabricated experts. It's about:
1. **Ingredient knowledge**: We know what's in our formulas and why
2. **Transparent communication**: We explain, not just claim
3. **Consistent premium aesthetics**: Every visual choice signals expertise
4. **Moroccan specificity**: We understand local skin concerns better than generic brands

## Authority Building Blocks

### 1. Ingredient Education
Each product page includes a dedicated "المكونات" (Ingredients) section that:
- Names each key ingredient in Arabic and English
- Explains its mechanism of action in simple Darija
- Specifies the concentration where appropriate
- Cites what it helps with (claim-safe language)

**Example (Niacinamide):**
> "الناياسيناميد — فيتامين B3 بصيغة فعّالة — مكوّن مدروس علمياً يساعد على توحيد مظهر البشرة وتخفيف اللمعان. نستخدموه بتركيز 10% في نيورا بالانس."

### 2. The "Why Nura" Section
Present on home and collection pages:
- "تركيبة مدروسة" — formulated with purpose
- "شفافية كاملة" — full ingredient transparency
- "مصنوعة للبشرة المغربية" — made for Moroccan skin
- "بدون إدعاءات زائفة" — no false claims

### 3. Science-Backed Aesthetics
- Ingredient names in both Arabic and INCI
- Clean, medical-adjacent typography
- White space and calm layout (not cluttered with claims)
- Measurement precision in copy ("10%", "50ml", "2–4 أيام")

### 4. Social Proof as Authority
- Customer testimonials with specific results described
- Named reviews (Moroccan names: Khadija, Meryem, Houda, etc.)
- Review counts ("اختارها +847 شخص هاد الشهر")
- Specific claim-safe outcomes: "بشرتي ولات أكثر نعومة بعد 3 أسابيع"

### 5. Brand Story as Authority
- Nama Beauty as the house behind Nura Skin
- "صنعناها لأن البشرة المغربية تستاهل عناية تفهمها"
- Journey from problem identification to formulation
- No fake founders, no fabricated dermatologists

## What We Never Do
- No fake before/after transformations
- No "dermatologist approved" claims without actual backing
- No "clinically proven" language without clinical data
- No guaranteed results ("سيعالج", "يزيل")
- No fake countdown timers or fake stock numbers
- No fabricated customer reviews

## Authority Voice Checklist
Before publishing any copy:
- [ ] Does it make a medical claim? → Soften to cosmetic language
- [ ] Does it cite a credential that doesn't exist? → Remove
- [ ] Does it explain *why* the ingredient works? → It should
- [ ] Does it sound like a Moroccan expert, not a translation? → It must
- [ ] Is the claim verifiable if challenged? → It must be
