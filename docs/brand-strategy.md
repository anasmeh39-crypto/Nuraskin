# Nura Skin — Brand Strategy

## Brand Identity

**Brand Name:** Nura Skin  
**Signature / House:** Nama Beauty  
**Domain:** nuraskin.cc  
**Market:** Morocco (primary), MENA (future)  
**Language:** Arabic Darija (primary), French (secondary, selective)  
**Business Model:** Direct-to-Consumer, Cash on Delivery only

---

## Brand Essence

> نيورا — العناية التي تفهم بشرتك.  
> *Nura — the care that understands your skin.*

Nura Skin is built on one belief: Moroccan women deserve skincare that speaks their language, understands their climate, and earns their trust without shortcuts.

---

## Positioning Statement

For Moroccan women aged 20–40 who care about their skin but are tired of generic imported products that don't account for Moroccan climate, skin tones, and daily realities — Nura Skin is a premium skincare brand that formulates with purpose and communicates with honesty.

Unlike international brands that translate packaging into Arabic, Nura Skin thinks and creates in the Moroccan context first.

---

## Brand Pillars

### 1. Honesty Over Hype
- No fake dermatologists, no guaranteed results, no before/after fraud
- Claim-safe cosmetic language throughout
- Science-backed ingredients, explained simply

### 2. Moroccan-First
- Copy in Darija, not formal MSA
- Reference real Moroccan skin concerns: sun, dry air, humidity, hammam rituals
- Pricing, delivery, and payment designed for Morocco

### 3. Premium Without Distance
- Looks and feels like a luxury brand
- Speaks like a trusted friend who knows skincare
- COD removes financial risk, building trust

### 4. Ritual Over Transaction
- Products are presented as rituals, not products
- Morning ritual, night ritual, eye ritual
- Encourages routine purchase, increases LTV

## Visual Identity Direction

### Color Palette
| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Deep Forest Green | `#1A3A2A` |
| Accent | Warm Gold | `#C9A84C` |
| Background | Warm Cream | `#FAF8F3` |
| Surface | White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Text Primary | Near Black | `#1C1C1E` |
| Text Secondary | Warm Gray | `#6B6B6B` |
| Border | Subtle Beige | `#E8E0D5` |

### Typography
- Arabic: Noto Naskh Arabic (body), Cairo (display/headings)
- Latin: Inter (UI), Playfair Display (logo/hero accents)
- Direction: RTL primary, LTR where Latin content appears

### Logo Concept
- Circle containing a stylized **N** (Nura)
- "نيورا سكين" below in Arabic
- "Nura Skin" and "Nama Beauty" in refined Latin below

### Photography Style
- Clean product photography on cream/white surfaces
- Moroccan texture elements (zellige, linen, natural stone)
- Skin close-ups (diverse Moroccan skin tones)
- Never generic stock imagery

---

## Claim Safety Policy

All copy must use cosmetic language, not medical claims:

| Forbidden | Allowed |
|---|---|
| "Cures acne" | "Helps reduce the appearance of blemishes" |
| "Clinically proven" | "Formulated with science-backed ingredients" |
| "Dermatologist approved" | "Developed with dermatological expertise" |
| "Removes wrinkles" | "Helps improve the appearance of fine lines" |
| "Treats dark circles" | "Helps reduce the appearance of dark circles" |

## Customer Promise

1. Your order arrives — COD, no upfront payment risk
2. The formula is honest — we explain every key ingredient
3. The routine is simple — 2–3 steps, not 12
4. We speak your language — literally and culturally
5. If something's wrong — we make it right (returns policy)
