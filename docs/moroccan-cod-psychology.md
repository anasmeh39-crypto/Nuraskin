# Moroccan COD Psychology

## The COD Reality in Morocco

Cash on Delivery is not a payment method in Morocco — it is a cultural contract.

The Moroccan ecommerce market is defined by:
- High distrust of online payment (card fraud fear, bank anxiety)
- Strong preference for tangible-first commerce (see before you pay)
- Community-driven purchasing decisions (word of mouth, peer validation)
- A generation that grew up with souks, negotiation, and tactile commerce

COD is how Morocco shops online. Any brand that doesn't offer it loses 60–80% of potential customers immediately.

---

## Why COD Destroys Brands That Don't Understand It

COD creates a specific operational challenge: **high cancellation rates**.

Customers who order on impulse, face delivery delays, or lose interest cancel at the door. This is the core challenge of Moroccan DTC:

**The cancellation rate problem:**
- Average Moroccan COD cancellation rate: 30–50% for generic brands
- Premium, trust-optimized brands: 10–20%
- Target for Nura Skin: <15%

Every element of the Nura Skin UI is designed to reduce this number.

## The COD Confidence System

### Pre-Order Trust
Before a customer places an order, they must believe:
1. The product is real
2. The brand is legitimate
3. The delivery will happen
4. They can refuse at the door if needed
5. They won't be pressured or scammed

**UI implementations:**
- Trust badges on product pages and checkout
- Transparent return and refusal policy language
- Social proof with recognizable Moroccan names/context
- Clear delivery timeline ("يوصلك خلال 2–4 أيام")
- COD badge prominently displayed everywhere

### Checkout Friction Reduction
The checkout popup must feel:
- Safe (not too many fields)
- Fast (name + phone only)
- Reassuring (summary + trust text visible)
- Professional (not a generic form)

**The 4 questions answered before submit:**
1. "غادي يجيني؟" → Delivery timeline shown
2. "واش غادي ندفع دابا؟" → COD explicitly stated
3. "واش هو مضمون؟" → Trust badges
4. "كيفاش غادي نعرف؟" → Confirmation communication implied

### The Cancellation Risk Window
The highest cancellation risk happens:
- T+0 to T+2 hours: Impulse cooling (post-order doubt)
- T+24 to T+48 hours: Delivery anxiety
- At door: Unexpected cost (shipping fee surprise)

**Counter-strategies built into UI:**
- Thank-you page designed to reinforce decision confidence
- Show order summary with shipping cost BEFORE order is placed
- Confirmation-first language: "طلبك وصل، شكراً على ثقتك"
- Soft upsell on thank-you reduces cancellation (they're invested)

## The Scarcity Framework (Authentic)

Moroccan customers have been conditioned to distrust fake scarcity. Only use:
- Real stock-based scarcity ("بقا غير 12 قطعة")
- Time-based offers that are genuine ("عرض خاص حتى نهاية الأسبوع")
- Bundle exclusivity ("هاد العرض مادايرناهوش حتى فالموقع")

Never use:
- Fake countdown timers that reset
- "Only 2 left" when stock is unlimited
- Flash sale language on evergreen products

## AOV Strategy in COD Context

COD markets have unique AOV dynamics:
- Higher AOV = higher cancellation risk (commitment anxiety)
- Lower AOV = lower margin, unsustainable
- **Sweet spot: 150–350 MAD per order**

AOV elevation strategies:
1. **Routine framing**: "الروتين الكامل" (the complete routine) — bundle the complete 4-product routine
2. **Starter + upgrade**: One product + "أضيفي هاد الخطوة لروتينك"
3. **Post-checkout upsell** (the only place for discounts): offer the companion product at 20–30% off
4. **Cart drawer cross-sells**: shown as "يكملها" (complements it)

## Phone Number as Trust Signal

In Morocco, the phone number field in checkout is sacred:
- Customers know they'll be called by delivery
- A phone call from a recognizable number = legitimate brand
- SMS confirmation builds trust in the brand
- Use Moroccan carrier formatting (06/07 prefixes)

**UX implementation:**
- Placeholder: "06XXXXXXXX"
- Validation: starts with 06 or 07, exactly 10 digits
- Inline validation: show green check when valid
- Error message in Darija: "رقم الهاتف غير صحيح، تأكدي من الصيغة"

## The Premium COD Paradox

Premium brands in Morocco face a tension:
- Premium = high price expectation
- COD = accessibility, low commitment signal

**Resolution:** Position COD as confidence, not accessibility.

Copy framing:
- ❌ "ادفع عند الاستلام لأن لا يوجد خيار آخر"
- ✅ "ما تدفعي حتى توصلك الطلبة وتشوفيها بعينيك"

The second framing positions COD as customer-respecting confidence, not a fallback.
