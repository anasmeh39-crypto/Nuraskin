# Nura Skin — Offers & Bundles System

## Offer Philosophy
Offers must feel premium and logical, not desperate.
The frame is always: "بناء روتين متكامل" (building a complete routine).
Discounts appear ONLY in the post-checkout upsell — never sitewide.

## Post-Checkout Upsell Structure

### Rules
- Shown ONCE after order confirmation, before thank-you
- No countdown and no fake urgency
- Discount: 20% off the companion product
- Copy must feel like routine completion, not pressure

### Logic
| Ordered | Upsell Offer |
|---|---|
| Any routine product without SPF | Daily Sunscreen SPF 50 at -20% |
| SPF only | Nura Balance at -20% |
| Full 4-product routine | No upsell — go directly to thank-you |

### Upsell Copy Template
```
أكملي روتينك بخطوة إضافية بسعر خاص

[Product Name]
[Arabic Name]

[2-line benefit in Arabic]

[Original Price] → [Discounted Price]
وفري [X] درهم

[أضيفيه لروتيني الآن]  [إكمال الطلب فقط]
```

## Cart AOV Levers

### Free Shipping Progress Bar
All orders have free delivery across Morocco.

### Cross-sell Widget in Cart
Shown when cart has 1–2 items:
- Title: "أكملي روتينك"
- Shows 1–2 products not in cart
- "+ أضيفي" button inline

### Bundle Upgrade in Cart
When individual products are in cart that form a bundle:
> "وفري [X] درهم بالطقم الكامل — اضغطي هنا"

## Offers Display Rules

| Element | Where Discounts Appear | Where They Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Sitewide sale | Never | — |
| Product page price | Regular price only | — |
| Collection page | Regular price only | — |
| Cart drawer | Bundle savings note (framed as bundle value) | No percentage off |
| Checkout popup | Regular price | No discount |
| Post-checkout upsell | Discounted price | — |
| Thank-you page | No offers | — |
