# Nura Skin — Market Positioning

## Competitive Landscape

### Direct Competitors (Moroccan DTC Skincare)
- Generic Facebook/TikTok shops with no brand identity
- Rebranded Chinese dropshipping products
- Local pharmacy brands (clinical but not premium)

### Indirect Competitors
- L'Oréal, Garnier (mass market, not Moroccan-specific)
- Kiehl's, Clarins (premium but inaccessible price, French-first)
- Local hammam/argan brands (traditional, not science-backed)

## Nura Skin's Positioning Gap
**The gap:** Premium + Science + Moroccan + COD + Arabic-first = nobody owns this space.

## The Positioning Formula
> "للمرأة المغربية التي تريد عناية جلدية علمية، ذات جودة عالية، تحترم ثقافتها وميزانيتها — بدون مخاطرة."
